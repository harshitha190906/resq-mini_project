How Leaky Bucket Controls Congestion

1. Burst Smoothing: In emergency situations, telemetry packets arrive in intense, unpredictable bursts (e.g. disaster triage flares). The Leaky Bucket buffers these spikes, converting irregular arrivals into a steady, constant-rate stream.

2. Downstream Protection: By leaking at a fixed rate ($\le$ bottleneck link bandwidth), subsequent microwave relays and routers are guaranteed never to experience queue overflow.

3. Controlled Overflow: If arrivals exceed capacity ($\text{Queue} + \text{Arriving} > \text{Capacity}$), excess packets are discarded immediately at the edge rather than congesting transit channels.
