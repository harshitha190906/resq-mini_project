Standard Network Metric Formulas

Delivery Ratio = (Delivered / Sent) × 100

Packet Loss Ratio = (Lost / Sent) × 100

Average Delay = Total Delay / Delivered Packets (ms)

Throughput = Delivered Bits / Duration (kbps)

Congestion Control Takeaway

Under low traffic loads (≤ 20 pkts/s), uncontrolled and Leaky Bucket traffic perform similarly. As disaster flash-crowd bursts exceed 40 pkts/s, uncontrolled links suffer 25–45% packet loss and elevated jitter due to tail-drop queue exhaustion, whereas Leaky Bucket paces egress to ensure zero transit router congestion.
